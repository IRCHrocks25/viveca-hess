What it's costing the firm

The leak is rarely where the firm is looking.

A practice can look sound from the outside and still lose hours every week it never gets back.

Intake that stalls. Handoffs held together by habit. Matters that move because someone remembered, not because a system moved them. Work that never needed a law degree quietly consumes the people you pay most to think. None of it shows up on a P&L as a line item. It shows up as capacity you assumed you didn't have.

Meanwhile AI may already be entering the firm through individual experimentation, embedded software features, client requests, and tools nobody has formally reviewed. Without a current picture of what is being used, by whom, and for what purpose, a firm cannot confidently manage confidentiality, supervision, data handling, client expectations, or an insurer's questions.

You can't govern what you haven't mapped. You can't fix a leak you haven't found.

How we see the whole firm

Operations and business development are the same money.

Operations advisers focus inside the firm.

Marketing advisers focus on how the work arrives.

Most firms are losing money at the seam between the two.

We look at both at once: how work moves through your firm, and how work comes to it. A referral pipeline producing more matters than intake can absorb isn't a growth win, it's a bottleneck with better PR. A workflow rebuilt without reference to how the firm actually wins business optimizes for the wrong thing. The value sits in the seam, and almost nobody is looking there.

The P.R.O.F.I.T. Method

A sequence, and the one exception to it.

Diagnosis before design. Governance before adoption. Adoption before investment.

Find where time, money, and opportunity are leaking, before any tool enters the conversation.

Where live exposure surfaces during Pinpoint, Fortify moves first. Everything else follows the sequence.

Most approaches run this backwards: tool first, questions later. That's how firms end up with expensive software, unclear return, and unmanaged exposure sitting quietly in the background.
